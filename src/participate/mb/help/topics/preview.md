# Preview

A top-down outline of the sculpture at the playhead.

- The six outer triangles sit at their clock positions and slide inward as their **linear** value grows.
- Each triangle’s fill and outline use the **rotary** lane color from the keyframe graph (e.g. Rot Twelve’s red).
- A stationary colored rail under each outer triangle shows that **linear** lane’s travel for the current animation (min→max extent), pointing inward, in the linear lane’s graph color.
- Each triangle rotates with its **rotary** value. The green dot marks the Aligned tip.
- The center box is rotary-only (its fill matches the center rotary lane color).
- Near a **Physics** problem at the playhead, the offending rotary fill or linear rail **pulses** brighter/darker in the same hue. A **pillar / tip** risk paints the tip’s third of the triangle (a diamond isolating that corner) in a harsh **magenta** pulse, 60% faster than the other warnings.

The preview uses the same blending as the sculpture, so what you see between keys is what will run.
