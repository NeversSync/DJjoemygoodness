# Workflows

## Create and submit an animation

1. **Pick a starting point**: Blank, or an existing show to remix. See [Starting points](#help:starting-points).
2. **Name it** in the toolbar (letters, numbers, `_` and `-` survive in the file name).
3. **Shape the motion**: scrub to a frame, adjust motor values or dials, or drag nodes in the [Keyframe graph](#help:keyframe-graph).
4. **Watch the preview** to see the boxes move. See [Preview](#help:preview).
5. **Fix physics issues** until the panel shows *Physics OK*. Use **Scale** / **Spread** (or **Scale All** / **Spread All**) as needed. See [Physics](#help:physics).
6. **Save JSON** to your device. See [Save & load](#help:save-load).
7. **Send the file to Our Team** (email or the site [booking / contact form](https://joemygoodness.com/#booking)). They review it and schedule it on the sculpture.

## Continue later

1. **Load JSON** and pick your saved file.
2. Keep editing, then **Save JSON** again (use a new name to keep versions).

## Remix a show

1. Pick a show under [Starting points](#help:starting-points) and press **Start**.
2. Rename it, then regroup motors in [Lanes](#help:groups) to make new symmetry (for example mirror halves with **Inv**).

## Clear a stubborn physics wall at the start

Frame 0 is Aligned and immovable. If a spike sits on the first frames, **Spread** expands the takeoff forward (keeps the peak value, lands it later). **Scale** instead shrinks that early travel. Prefer Spread when you want to keep the gesture; Scale when you want a smaller first move.
